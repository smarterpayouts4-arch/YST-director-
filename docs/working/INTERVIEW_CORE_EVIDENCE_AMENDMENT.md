# Proposed canon amendment — evidence-satisfiable CORE

**Status:** Proposed for human approval. Do not treat as live doctrine until copied into `project-knowledge/FEATURES/interview.md`.

**Why:** `FEATURES/interview.md` currently documents “usually ~5 core questions, hard max 7.” Evidence-satisfiable CORE coverage means a rich confirmed profile + selected strategic hypotheses can resolve some CORE information requirements without asking another question. That breaks the “~5 questions” contract even if the stage rail stays unchanged.

## Proposed replacement language

Replace the “usually ~5 core questions” line with:

> Five CORE *information requirements* must be resolved before the interview can complete: customer moment, viewer reward, business bridge, trust boundaries, and challenge assumption. A requirement is resolved by an owner answer, by an owner-selected strategic hypothesis that explicitly maps to the matching brief field, or by an explicit confirmed-profile field that already answers it without new model inference. Five questions are not required. Prefer information gain over questionnaire length. Hard max remains 7 questions.

## Out of scope for this amendment

- Collapsing the brief page or stage rail (separate, measurement-gated Step 7)
- Auto-rewriting `FEATURES/interview.md` (human approval required per `AGENTS.md`)
