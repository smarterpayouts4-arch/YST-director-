# Compelling Master Prompt Quality

The master prompt must be structurally complete **and** narratively compelling for a research agent.

## Required qualities

1. Opens with audience tension / customer moment
2. Uses context+conflict framing
3. Names a distinct educational story lens
4. Distinguishes fact / owner decision / hypothesis / research question / restriction
5. Requires disconfirming evidence and competitor classification
6. Ends with crisp DoD: 3 pillars, 6 experiments, 1 platform, CTA, success/failure criteria

## Out of scope

Asking for twenty generic topics or producing the research inside this app.
