# Story and Hook for Research Prompts

Make the **exported ChatGPT research prompt** audience-first and strategically sharp.

## Adopt

- **Dance:** context + conflict (“data suggests X, but Y is unclear”)
- **Curiosity with context:** questions connect to identifiable need
- **One-to-one tone:** strategist talking to one owner
- **Story lens:** unique educational angle, not generic category
- **Reward before promotion:** viewer value precedes company pitch

## Do not adopt (MVP)

- Scroll-retention / visual hooks as product features
- Short-form script generation
- Seven-scene video workflows
