# UX — Stage Rail and Ethical TARI Interview

## Stage rail

Narrow left rail listing the five product stages only:

1. Ingestion
2. Understanding
3. Interview
4. Research Brief
5. Final Prompt

The rail is orientation, not a dashboard of metrics. Current stage is highlighted; completed stages remain reachable for review when the state machine allows.

## Ethical TARI interview

Use Trigger → Action → Reward → Investment to reduce owner confusion — **not** to create compulsive behavior.

| Stage | Interview meaning |
|-------|-------------------|
| **Trigger** | Show a sharp observation grounded in company evidence |
| **Action** | Ask **one** material question |
| **Reward** | Reflect how the answer sharpens the research brief |
| **Investment** | Save into the growing brief |

### Rules

- One question on screen at a time.
- Prefer information gain over questionnaire length.
- Never gamify, variable-reward, or dark-pattern the owner.
- Owner can skip only when the product explicitly allows; skipped unknowns remain labeled.

## Composition

First viewport of each stage: one job, one primary CTA, calm typography. Avoid card clutter in the hero of a stage. Preserve trust: uploads are evidence for this project, not a marketing funnel.
